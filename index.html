Good questions

Wondering where to start?

It's completely fine if you don't know exactly what your website needs yet. Start with what your business does and what you want customers to know.

Ask us a question ↗
How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on the number of pages, design complexity, content, and features you need. Tell us about your goals and we can discuss a suitable scope and quote before work begins.

How long does it take to build a website?

Timelines depend on the size of the project and how quickly content and feedback are provided. We'll agree on a realistic schedule after discussing your requirements.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Responsive design is part of our approach. The layout is designed to adapt to common phone, tablet, and desktop screen sizes.

Do I need to buy a domain name?

A custom domain, such as yourbusiness.com, gives your website a memorable address. Hosting and domain registration are separate; we'll explain the options that fit your project.

Can you help if I don't know what I need?

Absolutely. Start by sharing what your business does, who you serve, and what you want visitors to do. We can help outline a practical first version.

Can you update an existing website?

Potentially, yes. Share your current website and the changes you're considering so we can understand the platform, scope, and best way forward.
